# 🏡 Places

Scenes people made and wanted to show. Every entry is a link that carries the whole place inside itself, so it keeps working as long as the app can read it.

## Add yours

[**Submit a place**](../../issues/new?template=place.yml) — press **Store** in the app's Send panel, attach the postcard, and it can be added here.

Two things worth knowing before you do:

- **The Store link, not the short one.** The Send panel offers both. **Copy** makes the short link, which points at a row on the server that is deleted after 30 days. **Store** copies the whole scene inside the address itself, and that one does not expire. A gallery built from short links would fall apart within a month.
- **Attach your postcard.** A link cannot preview as the place it opens — GitHub, like every chat app, never receives the part of a URL after `#` — so the picture is what makes a gallery a gallery rather than a row of identical cards.
